About us

We started with a simple question: how often do you feel like you have nothing to wear, even with a wardrobe full of clothes? The answer we heard most: “all the time.” That’s why we are building Attira — to help you rediscover outfits from pieces you already own (and perhaps forgot about), reduce decision making, and make getting dressed feel effortless and fun again. We want to turn your wardrobe into a catalogue of possibilities you didn’t even know you had.
